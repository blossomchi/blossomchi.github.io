Tutorials and teaching materials

Coding lessons, beginner-friendly demos and AI and robotics training for teens and kids.

Overview

These are the materials from my work teaching kids and teens at the annual AI boot camp and on my YouTube channel, Blossom Coding World. Over the years I have trained more than 100 students in Python, robotics, DIY drones and AI concepts, using practical examples so that hard ideas become approachable.
